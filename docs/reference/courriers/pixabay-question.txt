Subject: License question - photos used inside an offline recipe application


Hello,

I would like to confirm one point of the Pixabay Content License before
shipping, rather than after.

I am building a free, offline recipe and meal-planning application. It has
no server, no account, no tracking and no ads. Ten of the recipe photos it
ships come from Pixabay, each credited to its author in a credits file.

Section 5 of your Terms distinguishes two cases, and I want to be sure I am
reading it the way you intend. It says that resizing or cropping alone
remains Standalone use, but that using Content "with a combination of
images, videos, audio files, other media, text, illustrations, background
features and editing techniques is not Standalone use, so long as the
combined effect is to make a 'new' creative work."

Here is exactly how each photo appears in the app:

- It always sits inside a recipe page, next to the recipe name, the
  ingredient list with quantities, the preparation steps, the cooking and
  preparation times, and the portion count. It is never shown on its own.
- The app has no gallery, no image browser, no download, no export and no
  save function. There is no screen where a photo can be viewed by itself.
- Each photo is re-encoded to AVIF and capped at 1024 px wide. I understand
  from your terms that this alone does not change the answer, and I am not
  relying on it.
- Attribution and licence are shipped with the app, in a credits file, for
  every photo.

The photos are part of the application package rather than loaded from a
server, because the app must work fully offline with no connection at all.
That is a core constraint of the project, not a preference.

So my question is narrow: does a photo used this way fall under the
"combination ... new creative work" case, rather than Standalone use?

If it does not, I will remove the Pixabay photos and say so. I would rather
ask now than be wrong later.

Thank you for your time.
